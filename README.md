# Eli & Elyn Finance Dashboard

A self-contained household finance dashboard for expenses, bills, savings goals, and investment tracking.

## Open it

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## What it includes

- Monthly income, spending, bills, savings rate, and net worth KPIs
- Category budgets with live progress
- Recurring bill calendar with paid toggles
- Savings goals and investment account progress
- Charts for cash flow, expense mix, goal progress, and asset mix
- Add-entry modal for expenses, bills, income, goal contributions, and investment contributions
- Browser localStorage persistence with a reset button
